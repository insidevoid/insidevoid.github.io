Privacy policy – Arun Invoice

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Arun Invoice is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

It uses the Google permission drive.file. This lets it open only the one invoice template the owner picked himself, plus the temporary copies it creates. It cannot see any other file in the Google Drive. It also reads the account's email address to confirm the right account is signed in.

What it does with the data

It copies the template, fills in the invoice details the owner provides, exports a PDF to the owner's own computer, and deletes the temporary copy. Data is not sold, shared, sent to third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can remove access at any time in the Google account's security settings.
